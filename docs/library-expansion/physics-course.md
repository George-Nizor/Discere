# Physics: Motion and Forces

Published: 2 October 2026. Course ID: `physics-motion-and-forces`, version 1.0.0.

Eighteen connected lessons introduce one-dimensional motion, forces, work, energy and momentum.
The course contains 108 lesson questions, 36 fresh recall cards, eighteen concepts and 72 answered
visual beats with 144 authored cases. Three separate checks contribute another 54 problems.

This is partial coverage of Mechanics in George's
[Notion study map](https://app.notion.com/p/3ebea4c04f4c813db47dc7dc22e8f357).
It does not complete Physics. Two-dimensional motion, rotation, torque, circular motion,
gravitation, oscillations, fluids and wider mechanics remain; thermodynamics and quantum mechanics
remain separate unimplemented subjects.

## Sequence and learning behavior

| Module | Lessons |
| --- | --- |
| Describing motion | Where did it go?; Speed and direction; Read a motion graph |
| Changing motion | Changing velocity; Predict the next position; Falling and rising |
| Forces on an object | When forces balance; Force, mass and acceleration; Weight and support |
| Objects that interact | Forces come in pairs; Friction that adjusts; When a force does work |
| Following energy | Energy of motion; Energy and height; Where the energy goes |
| Rates and conservation | Power and efficiency; Momentum and impulse; When carts stick together |

Signed numbers, ratios, basic algebra and square roots are prerequisites. Every lesson asks four
visual teaching questions before revealing the corresponding explanation, followed by two skills
questions and two new recall problems. Seventy-two questions use numeric marking; 36 use choices.
Prompts state units, signs, gravitational acceleration and idealizing assumptions.

The shared player gives earned sound and a green frame after a correct answer. A mistake opens
its correction and allows continuation while preserving the failed first response. Fresh recall
tests another problem; revealing a correction does not manufacture independent mastery.

The three course checks each cover all eighteen lessons. Placement is optional. The mixed check
requires actual completion of all lesson stages. Delayed applications become available seven
elapsed days after the mixed check, and appear in Review. First responses and confidence are saved;
correctness is concealed until submission of the full set. Each check currently has one form.

## Models and drawings

Fourteen bounded model kinds supply original SVGs: journeys, constant-acceleration motion,
velocity changes, force sums, support forces, interaction pairs, friction, work, kinetic energy,
height changes, energy descent, power, impulse and sticking collisions.

Models distinguish distance from displacement and speed from signed velocity. Static friction
adjusts below its limit; sliding friction stops a coasting box without accelerating it backward.
Support force follows the stated acceleration. Momentum is conserved only under the stated
negligible-external-impulse condition, while a sticking collision can convert kinetic energy.

Comparisons share position, force or energy scales. Graphs join exact authored observations.
Cart icons in two-body diagrams occupy offset rows on one shared horizontal position scale so
their labels remain legible, including after contact. The cover is an original blue cart, gold
trajectory and green arrow, separate from the technical diagrams.

Timed models have finite playback, pause, reset and keyboard scrubbing. Manual and system reduced
motion stop playback immediately while retaining manual steps. Work, lifting and energy descent
use a progress slider; the descent's prescribed heating progression is not a solved time history.
Worked numerical results remain hidden until grading and are labelled for the full interval.
