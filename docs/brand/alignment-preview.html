Discere · brand v2 alignment · proposal for approval

Same game, Instrumenta's hand

Discere takes the suite's type, its icon style and its depth, and keeps everything that makes it feel like a game: XP, quests, combos, chests and Bonehead. Nothing in the app changes until you approve this sheet. Lesson content (maths, diagrams, maps) keeps its own type.

Type

Fraunces for the moments: page titles, the greeting, course and lesson names, level-ups, the finish screen. Commissioner for everything you read to operate the app, including questions. Spline Sans Mono for numbers that count: XP, quest counts, combos, code and answers in mono.

Questions stay in Commissioner, not Fraunces: a serif headline on every question would slow reading.

Buttons

The icons' extrusion, made pressable: an ink outline and a deep edge down and to the right. A press pushes the button into its edge. Blue for actions, green only for "correct", gold only for rewards.

This keeps the chunky game feel of the current buttons and matches the suite's depth.

Icons

Drawn with the brand library's own renderer: a flat object, an ink outline and the stepped extrusion, at three levels of detail (48, 24, 16 px). Each has a meaning colour: blue for Discere, gold for rewards, ember for heat, violet for memory, teal for leagues and tools, green only for done.

They replace the line icons in navigation, the top bar, quests, the chest, achievements, items and the lesson toolbar. Small utility marks (close, arrows, chevrons) stay as simple lines.

Hover an icon to see it move. In the app each would move on a meaningful moment: the chest lid on opening, the flame on a streak, the tick on a finished quest.

Home

The book logo in the top bar, Bonehead in the hero, Fraunces for the greeting and panel titles, branded icons for stats and quests, mono numbers.

What I need from you

Approve, or tell me what to change. The answers set the build.

  1. Type: Fraunces for titles and moments, Commissioner for the interface and questions, mono for numbers. Yes?
  2. Buttons: the outlined, extruded style above, replacing the current flat-with-shadow buttons everywhere.
  3. Icons: this set in place of the line icons across navigation, stats, quests, items, achievements and the lesson toolbar. Any icon you would redraw?
  4. Answer choices raised like buttons, with mono text for maths and code answers.
  5. Kept as they are: course cover art, Bonehead, the aurora, lesson diagrams and maths typesetting, the dark theme.
Mock screens are built in HTML from the proposed styles; they are not screenshots. Icons are rendered by the same algorithm as the Instrumenta icon library, so they will look identical in the app.
