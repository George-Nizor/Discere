# Open sources for new courses

Collected on 14 November 2026 by an agent, partly from Atlas Open Academy's citations and library. Licence tags say how each was checked; confirm a licence on the publisher's own page before copying text into a source pack. Atlas Open Academy's own lesson text carries no reuse licence: use it as a map of topics and of what it cites, never as a source to copy.

NonCommercial (NC) licences suit Discere, which is private and not sold; ShareAlike (SA) means adapted text keeps the same licence; NoDerivatives (ND) means cite only.

## How to read the licence tags

| Tag | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| [V] | I saw the licence on the publisher's own page or API today. |
| [A] | Licence as labelled in Atlas's Library data (https://atlasopenacademy.org/assets/js/library-data.js). I did not open the book's own page. |
| [O] | Licence as listed by the Open Textbook Library (https://open.umn.edu), not checked on the book itself. |
| [R] | Reported by a web search result. Treat as probable and confirm before copying text. |
| [K] | From general knowledge, not fetched today. |

Licence terms in short: CC BY and CC0 and public domain allow copying and adapting with credit (or without). CC BY-SA also lets you adapt, but your adapted text must carry the same licence. CC BY-NC and BY-NC-SA bar commercial use; a private app is fine on that, but a ShareAlike adaptation stays CC BY-NC-SA if the app is ever shared. ND (no derivatives) means cite it, do not adapt it. Safest plan for all NC-SA books: use them as reading sources and write our own lesson text from the ideas, rather than pasting or closely paraphrasing.

Important correction to common belief: OpenStax's current editions are no longer CC BY. OpenStax's own CMS (https://openstax.org/apps/cms/api/v2/pages/?type=books.Book&fields=title,slug,license_name,book_state&limit=100) gives CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 for the "live" editions of Economics 3e, Sociology 3e, Anthropology, Political Science, American Government 4e, World History vols 1 and 2, US History, Psychology 2e, Principles of Accounting 1 and 2, Principles of Finance 2e, Introduction to Business 2e, Business Ethics, Nutrition for Nurses and most others. Only Physics (high school) and Statistics are still CC BY 4.0. Retired earlier editions (Economics 2e, Sociology 2e, American Government 3e, Psychology 1e) show CC BY 4.0, and a CC BY grant already given cannot be withdrawn. The Open Textbook Library still lists Sociology 3e as CC BY, which looks out of date.

## What I could not fetch or confirm

- smarthistory.org, pressbooks.pub (poetry book), openfl.pressbooks.pub (World Religions), perspectives pressbooks, bogleheads.org: all returned HTTP 403 to my fetcher. Their licences above come from search results, tagged [R].
- metmuseum.org returned HTTP 429; the CC0 statement comes from search results.
- openstax.org book pages render in JavaScript, so I used OpenStax's own CMS API for licences instead. That is a reliable source for OpenStax but I did not read each book's copyright page.
- The Open Textbook Library subject pages came back as cached first pages only (10 titles each), so religion, anthropology and constitutional law listings are incomplete.
- Cornell's Wex text licence: the LII terms page does not name Wex.
- Licences tagged [A] are Atlas's labels. They look careful (each has a licence URL), but I verified only OpenStax, the University of Hawai'i nutrition book, Our World in Data and the LII terms myself.
- I did not read the Atlas courses' interactive data under /assets/js/courses/ (robots.txt disallows it).
