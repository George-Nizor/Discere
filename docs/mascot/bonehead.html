Bonehead, ready for lessons

The Bonehead Labs bone, redrawn in code so it can blink, wobble, listen and sleep. The silhouette, outline weight, headphones and teal puddle are kept as they are; the changes are a face that can emote, a rim for the dark interface and a heavier mark for favicon sizes. Move the cursor and it watches. Click it. Leave it alone and it dozes, headphones round its neck.

Faithful to the original

The brand image, the code redraw, and the two laid over each other. The redraw is symmetric where the original drifts by a pixel or two, so it can be mirrored and animated cleanly.

Expressions

The original face (oval eyes, small smile) is the idle state. The others change the eyes and mouth, and the whole bone leans, stretches or squashes with them. Asleep, the headphones slip down round its neck.

Moments

Headphone behaviours and the optional props, each tied to one moment in a lesson.

Sizes and silhouette

From 48 px down, the mark variant thickens the outline and enlarges the eyes. The tall bone fills about two thirds of a square, so at 16 px the headphones and the white body carry it more than the face does. The silhouette column is one flat colour.

Marks and lockups

The mark alone, then two wordmark lockups: Bone Up (proposed platform name: to bone up on something is to study it hard) and Bonehead Labs. The wordmarks use the system font stack here; a final version would be outlined to paths.

In a lesson

The pet docked in the corner of a lesson. It reacts to answers, listens while the lesson is read aloud, and can be tucked away.

Treatment

Rules it follows here

  • Sits in the corner outside the reading column; never covers a choice or the Check button.
  • Reacts once per answer, then settles. Nothing for visiting a screen.
  • Right: ear cups light up teal. Wrong: a wobble and encouragement, never a sad face.
  • Read aloud: it nods along with its headphones on.
  • Sleeps after a pause, headphones round its neck; wakes on the next input.
  • Reduced motion keeps every expression and drops the movement.
