Companion concept sheet · round 1

Four candidates for the companion

Each one is drawn in code: inline SVG, no image files. Move the cursor and they watch it. Click one to pet it. Answer right or wrong and they react. Leave them alone for seven seconds and they fall asleep. None of them depends on the platform's name.

At a glance

Species, temperament and colour story. The accent colours are chosen to sit on the app's near-black canvas and to stand alone as a mark.

Playground

Click a companion to pet it (keep clicking). Correct and wrong answers trigger the reactions a lesson would use. The thin bar counts down to sleep; any input wakes them.

Expressions

The six states every surface draws from. Body language changes with the face: antennae, leaves, quills or the ring tilt with the mood.

Sizes and silhouette

The mark variant drops highlights, blush and small strokes so it holds at favicon size. The silhouette column is the honest test: one flat colour, no detail.

Logo mark, no wordmark

Transparent canvas, no container, as the suite's brand rules ask. Shown on transparency, suite ink and paper.

In a lesson

The pet docked in the corner of a lesson. It reacts to answers, says very little, and can be tucked away.

Companion

Rules it follows here

  • Sits in the corner outside the reading column; never covers a choice or the Check button.
  • Reacts once per answer, then settles. No reaction to visiting a screen.
  • A wrong answer earns encouragement, never a sad face.
  • Sleeps after a pause; wakes on the next input.
  • Reduced motion keeps every expression and drops the movement.
