# Figure wishlist: chemistry-atoms-to-reactions

Figures the first five lessons would use if the course had the diagram type. Each screen teaches without
its figure. The course only has `chemistry_explorer`, which shows fixed cases and cannot be changed by the learner.

## First five lessons: drawn

Drawn as `svg_figure`s (what-makes-an-element opener and `inside-one-atom`, isotopes-and-mass opener,
`working-backwards` and `average-mass`, ions-and-charge opener and `plus-or-minus`, outer-electrons opener
and `full-shells`, neutral-ionic-formulas opener, `a-neutral-crystal` and `when-charges-differ`). The
sliders and steppers the writers asked for became buttons (cases). Left as they were: the chemistry
explorer figures that already match their text. Still wished for: `the-layers` in outer-electrons, a seat plan
for atoms 1 to 18, which the explorer covers only for two atoms.

## sharing-electron-pairs

- **Step `covalent-bond`.** Two atoms start apart, each with its outer electrons drawn as dots. A slider brings them together and the unpaired electrons join into one pair between the nuclei, with the shell count of each atom updating from 1 to 2 for hydrogen. The learner can switch to two chlorine atoms and watch each count go from 7 to 8. Caption: "Move the atoms together and watch each one count the shared pair."
- **Step `more-pairs`.** A Lewis drawing builder for Cl₂, O₂, N₂, CO₂ and ethene. The learner adds a line between two atoms and the shared-electron total and each atom's count update. The course's Lewis figure only has H₂, O₂, N₂, H₂O, NH₃ and CH₄, so CO₂ and ethene have no drawing. Caption: "Add lines between the atoms and read off the shared and unshared electrons."

## reading-chemical-formulas

- **Step `a-whole-order`.** A row of lunch boxes labelled with a formula such as H₂O, with a stepper for the number in front and a highlight that shows the subscript counting inside each box and the coefficient counting boxes. Species the course lacks, such as H₂SO₄ and C₂H₆, could be added to it. Caption: "Change the number in front and see how many of each atom the order holds."

## adding-atomic-masses

- **Step `several-elements`.** A stacked bar for one formula where each element is a block of height equal to its count times its atomic mass. The learner picks the formula from a list, including C₆H₁₂O₆ and CaCO₃, and the bar totals the formula mass. Caption: "Pick a formula and see each element's share of the total mass."

## from-moles-to-mass

- **Step `a-dozen-for-atoms`.** A scale with a dozen eggs, a dozen bolts and a mole of two different substances on it, each labelled with its count and its mass, to show that equal counts weigh different amounts. The learner picks the substance. Caption: "The same count weighs a different amount for each substance."
- **Step `back-to-moles`.** A scale reading in grams beside a gauge in moles. The learner types or drags a mass and the gauge shows n = m ÷ M for a chosen substance. Caption: "Change the mass on the scale and read the moles off the gauge."

## balancing-reactions

- **Step `where-to-start`.** An atom tally table with a column for each side of an equation and a coefficient stepper beside each formula. Rows turn green as an element matches. It needs equations the course's reaction figure does not hold, such as propane burning and sodium with chlorine. Caption: "Step the coefficients until every row turns green."

## reaction-mole-ratios

- **Step `other-amounts`.** A recipe scaler for 2 K + Br₂ → 2 KBr with a slider for the amount of K. The conversion-factor fraction fills in as the slider moves, the units cancel visibly, and a bar shows the Br₂ amount. The learner can switch to the pancake recipe and see the same fraction. Caption: "Move the slider and watch the fraction turn an amount of K into an amount of Br₂."
- **Step `either-direction`.** The methane equation with a pair picker. The learner picks the substance they have and the one they want, and the fraction appears the right way up with the answer for a chosen amount. Caption: "Pick two substances and read the fraction in either direction."

## the-reactant-that-runs-out

- **Step `a-batch-of-a-reaction`.** Two bins of reactant molecules for 2 H₂ + O₂ → 2 H₂O with a stepper for each supply. Pressing Run takes one batch at a time from both bins until one is empty, then highlights the limiting reactant and shows the leftovers. Caption: "Set the two supplies and press Run to see which one gives out first."
- **Step `counting-sandwiches`.** The same Run animation with bread and cheese, so the learner sees the idea before it meets a chemical. Caption: "Change the bread and the cheese and watch the sandwiches stop."
