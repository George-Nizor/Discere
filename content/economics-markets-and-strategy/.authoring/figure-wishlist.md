# Figures wished for

Economics: Markets and Strategy, lessons 1 to 5. Each screen below teaches without the figure.

## Lesson 1, `opportunity-cost-and-the-frontier`

- Step `what-a-choice-costs`. A budget line for the £20 afternoon, with the paperback and the bubble tea as the two axes. The learner can change the price of the book and watch the number of teas it replaces change. Caption: "Every £8 spent on the book is two bubble teas you can't have."

## Lesson 4, `demand-and-supply`

- Step `buyers-and-price`. A demand curve on its own, before supply is introduced. The learner can drag a price marker and read the quantity demanded. Caption: "The lower the price, the more lollies people plan to buy." The existing market figure always draws both curves, so the screen has no figure.
- Step `demand-as-an-equation`. The same demand curve with its intercept (60) and slope (5) marked, and a slider that changes the slope. Caption: "A steeper change in quantity per pound means buyers react more strongly to price."
- Step `sellers-and-price`. A supply curve on its own, with a price marker. Caption: "The higher the price, the more lollies sellers plan to offer."

## Lesson 7, `consumer-and-producer-surplus`

- Step `what-a-ticket-is-worth`. A row of bars, one per buyer, each showing that buyer's willingness to pay against a price line. The learner can drag the price line and watch which buyers buy and how big each gap is. Caption: "Each buyer's surplus is the part of their bar above the price."

## Lesson 8, `taxes-and-deadweight-loss`

- Step `the-wedge`. A single price scale with two markers, the price buyers pay and the price sellers keep, and the tax as the gap between them. The learner can slide the buyers' price and watch the sellers' price move with it. Caption: "Whatever buyers pay, sellers keep £2 less." The existing tax view needs a market with a full equilibrium, so the screen has no figure.

## Lesson 10, `costs-and-profit`

- Step `two-kinds-of-cost`. A stacked bar for total cost with the fixed part and the variable part in different shades, and a slider for the number of posters printed. Caption: "The £50 rent stays the same height at every output, and the paper and ink grow."
- Step `one-more-unit`. A table of total cost by output with a marginal-cost column that fills in as the learner steps through the outputs. Caption: "Each marginal cost is the new total minus the one before."
