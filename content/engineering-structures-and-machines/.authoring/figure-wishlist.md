# Figures wished for: Engineering lessons 1 to 5

Every screen below teaches without the figure. It uses `engineering_explorer` where one fits, and the entries here are
what would help more. Nothing in the app was changed for them.

## Lesson 1: forces-as-vectors

### a-pull-at-a-slant
- **Shows:** a post with a rope running up to a tree, and beside it the same pull drawn as an across arrow and an up arrow.
- **Learner changes:** the angle of the rope, with both arrows resizing.
- **Caption:** "Tilt the rope and watch the two pieces trade size."

## Lesson 2: moments

### line-of-action
- **Shows:** a spanner with the line of action of the push drawn as a long dashed line and the perpendicular distance from the bolt marked as a short right-angled segment.
- **Learner changes:** the angle of the push, and whether the line passes through the bolt.
- **Caption:** "The perpendicular distance shrinks to nothing when the line passes through the bolt."

## Lesson 3: supported-beam

### pins-and-rollers
- **Shows:** a pin and a roller side by side, each with arrows for the movements it allows and the pushes it can give.
- **Learner changes:** the direction of a push on each support.
- **Caption:** "The pin can push in any direction. The roller can only push at right angles to its surface."

## Lesson 4: trusses-by-joints

### push-or-pull
- **Shows:** one bar between two joints, with arrows on the joints for a pull and for a push, labelled tension and compression.
- **Learner changes:** the direction of the force on the bar.
- **Caption:** "A bar that pulls its joints together is in tension. One that pushes them apart is in compression."

### the-apex-joint
- **Shows:** the apex joint cut out of the truss, with the load and the two member forces drawn as arrows and their upward parts marked.
- **Learner changes:** the height of the apex.
- **Caption:** "The two upward parts must add up to the load."

## Lesson 5: stress-and-strain

### sharing-a-load
- **Shows:** one rod and three rods carrying the same load, with the force on each rod and its cross-section drawn to scale.
- **Learner changes:** the number of rods and their thickness.
- **Caption:** "More steel in the cross-section means less force on each square millimetre."

### stretch-as-a-fraction
- **Shows:** a bar with its original length and its stretched length marked, and the extension drawn enlarged.
- **Learner changes:** the original length and the extension.
- **Caption:** "Strain is the extension as a fraction of the original length."

# Figures wished for: Engineering lessons 6 to 10

Every screen below teaches without the figure. It uses `engineering_explorer` where one fits, and the entries here are
what would help more. Nothing in the app was changed for them.

## Lesson 6: factor-of-safety

### how-much-room-is-left
- **Shows:** a bar of stress drawn as a scale, with the working stress as a marker and the yield stress as a line above it, and the gap between them shaded and labelled with the factor.
- **Learner changes:** the load, with the marker rising towards the yield line.
- **Caption:** "The factor of safety is the yield stress divided by the working stress."

## Lesson 7: shear-and-moment

### sawing-the-plank
- **Shows:** the plank cut in two at a movable point, with the left piece drawn on its own and arrows for the post, the crate if it is on that piece, and the force and turning effect on the cut face.
- **Learner changes:** the position of the cut.
- **Caption:** "The cut face supplies whatever force and turning effect keep the piece at rest."

## Lesson 8: bending-stress

### squeezed-above-stretched-below
- **Shows:** a beam cross-section beside a row of arrows, pointing inwards above the neutral axis and outwards below it, with their lengths growing with distance from the axis.
- **Learner changes:** the depth of the section and the bending moment.
- **Caption:** "Stress is zero at the neutral axis and largest at the outer fibre."

## Lesson 9: deflection-and-stiffness

### load-per-millimetre
- **Shows:** a graph of tip load against tip deflection for a cantilever, a straight line through zero, with its slope marked as the stiffness.
- **Learner changes:** the length of the cantilever, which tilts the line.
- **Caption:** "Stiffness is the slope: the load for each millimetre of deflection."

## Lesson 10: levers-and-pulleys

### what-the-saving-costs
- **Shows:** a lever with both ends drawn at rest and after a push, with the distance moved by the effort and by the load marked, and beside it a pulley block with the rope pulled out and the load lifted.
- **Learner changes:** the position of the fulcrum, and the number of strands.
- **Caption:** "The end with the smaller force moves further. The work in and the work out stay equal."

## Lesson 11: gear-trains

### chains-and-sprockets
- **Shows:** a chain looping round a large chainring and a small sprocket, with the teeth passing a marked point on each and an arrow for each wheel's direction of turning.
- **Learner changes:** the number of teeth on the sprocket, with both speeds updating.
- **Caption:** "The chain carries teeth past both wheels at the same rate, so the smaller wheel turns faster."

## Lesson 12: efficiency-and-power

### turning-speed-in-radians
- **Shows:** a wheel with a string as long as its radius laid along the rim, and the angle it covers at the centre marked as one radian.
- **Learner changes:** the radius of the wheel, and how many radians of rim are laid out.
- **Caption:** "The string covers one radian however big the wheel is."

### where-the-power-goes
- **Shows:** a bar for the power going into a gearbox that splits at each mesh into a bar that carries on and a smaller bar marked as heat.
- **Learner changes:** the efficiency of each mesh and the number of meshes.
- **Caption:** "Each mesh passes on a fraction of what it receives, so the bar shrinks at every step."
