# Figures the first five lessons would use

Each screen teaches without the figure. The lessons use only `geometry_explorer` cases that exist today.

| Lesson | Step | What it shows | What the learner can change | Caption |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| measuring-turns | counting-quarter-turns | A top-down view of a person on a spot with a door ahead. Quarter, half and whole turns are drawn as arcs | A slider that turns the person | Drag to turn. Two quarter-turns make a half-turn and four make a whole turn. |
| angles-in-triangles | three-corners-on-a-line | A triangle whose three corners come off and slide onto a straight line, where they fit together | A button that tears the corners off, and a choice of triangle | Tear off the corners and set them along the line. They always fill it. |
| boundary-and-area | tiling-the-floor | A rectangle covered with unit tiles, filled in row by row with a running count | Length and width sliders | Each row adds the same number of tiles. |
| base-and-height | the-area-of-any-triangle | A leaning triangle inside the rectangle that has the same base and height, with the two leftover pieces shaded | A slider that moves the top corner along its line | The triangle is always half of the rectangle, wherever the corner sits. |
| pieces-and-cutouts | adding-pieces | An L-shaped floor cut into two rectangles, then the same L drawn as a big rectangle with the corner removed | A switch between the two views | Both ways give the same area. |
| pieces-and-cutouts | two-trapezoids-make-a-parallelogram | A trapezoid, a flipped copy, and the two joined into a parallelogram | A slider that slides the copy into place | Two trapezoids make one parallelogram. |

Figures for the first five lessons are drawn (measuring-turns, angles-in-triangles, boundary-and-area, base-and-height, pieces-and-cutouts). Nothing is left from that group; existing geometry_explorer diagrams stay where they already fit.

# Figures the next five lessons would use

Each screen teaches without the figure. The lessons use only `geometry_explorer` cases that exist today.

| Lesson | Step | What it shows | What the learner can change | Caption |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| around-and-inside-circles | the-distance-round | A string wrapped once round a tin, then straightened beside a ruler, with the tin's width marked on the same ruler | A choice of tin, from a mug to a wheel | The string is always a little more than three widths long. |
| around-and-inside-circles | the-space-inside | A circle with a square drawn on its radius, then the circle filled with copies of that square, three whole ones and a sliver | A slider for the radius | The circle holds a little over three radius-squares. The exact number is π. |
| when-area-scales | covering-a-bigger-tile | A small square tile and a bigger copy, with the big tile covered by small tiles in rows and a running count | A slider for the scale factor, from 1 to 4 | Three tiles along each edge and three rows make nine. |
| right-triangle-distances | squares-on-the-sides | A right triangle with a square drawn on each side, the two small squares cut into unit squares and the large one filled by the same pieces | Leg sliders, and a button that moves the pieces across | The two small squares always fill the large one exactly. |
| distance-on-a-grid | either-side-of-zero | A grid with both axes, two points on opposite sides of zero, and the gap drawn as a bar that crosses zero with each part labelled | Dragging either point | A gap that crosses zero is the two distances to zero added together. |

# Figures for the last two lessons

Each screen teaches without the figure. The lessons use only `geometry_explorer` cases that exist today.

| Lesson | Step | What it shows | What the learner can change | Caption |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| filling-space | stacking-cubes | A box filled with unit cubes, one layer at a time, with a running count of cubes for each layer added | A slider for the number of layers, and one for the cubes in a row | Each layer adds the same number of cubes. |
| unfolding-surface-area | flatten-the-box | A closed box that opens out flat into its net, with each pair of matching faces shaded in one colour | A button that unfolds and folds the box | Cut along the edges and the six faces lie flat, in three matching pairs. |
