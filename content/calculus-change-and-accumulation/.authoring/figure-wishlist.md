# Figure wishlist: calculus-change-and-accumulation

Figures the lessons would use if the course had them. Each screen teaches without its figure.

## from-secant-to-tangent / the-derivative

- Shows: the curve y = x² with its tangent, and below it a second graph that plots the tangent slope against x as the tangent moves.
- The learner changes: the point of tangency.
- Caption: "Slide the tangent. The lower graph records its slope at each position."

## position-velocity-acceleration / acceleration

- Shows: the position curve of the car s(t) = 4t² with a second graph below it on the same time axis showing its velocity v(t) = 8t, and a third below that showing the acceleration, with a marker at the same time on all three.
- The learner changes: the time, by dragging one marker through all three graphs.
- Caption: "Drag the marker. Each graph shows the slope of the one above it."

## position-velocity-acceleration / which-way-it-goes

- Shows: a toy car on a short track beside the position graph s(t) = t² − 8t, with an arrow on the car whose direction and length show the sign and size of the velocity.
- The learner changes: the time, with a play button.
- Caption: "Press Play. The arrow points the way the car moves, and its length is the speed."

## when-a-curve-turns / top-of-a-throw

- Shows: a ball's height graph h(t) = 10t − t² with a strip beneath it that is green where the slope is positive, grey at zero and red where it is negative.
- The learner changes: the time, by dragging a marker along the curve.
- Caption: "Drag the marker over the top. The strip shows the sign of the slope."

## when-a-curve-turns / ends-of-an-interval

- Shows: the curve y = x² − 4x with the interval [0, 5] shaded, the two ends and the stationary point marked, and a table of the three heights beside it.
- The learner changes: the two ends of the interval.
- Caption: "Move the ends. The highest and lowest of the three heights are marked."

## undoing-a-derivative / many-functions-one-derivative

- Shows: a family of curves F(x) = 3x² + C stacked at different heights, with one tangent drawn at the same x on every curve to show that the slopes match.
- The learner changes: x, and the constant C, and a pin that fixes one curve through a chosen point.
- Caption: "Every curve has the same slope at the same x. Pin one to choose C."

## rectangles-that-refine / thinner-strips

- Shows: the area under y = 2x on [0, 3] with a strip count from 1 to 24, the left, right and midpoint sums side by side and a bar for the gap between the left and right sums.
- The learner changes: the number of strips.
- Caption: "Add strips and watch the gap between the left and right sums close."

## area-with-a-sign / filling-and-draining

- Shows: a rate graph that is positive for the first stretch and negative for the second, with a water-level graph below it that rises, then falls, on the same time axis.
- The learner changes: the two rates and how long each lasts.
- Caption: "Change the rates. The level graph below keeps the running total."

## the-two-ideas-connect / a-running-total

- Shows: the rate graph r(x) = 2x on top and, beneath it on the same x-axis, the running total A(x) = x², with a marker at the same x on both and the area up to the marker shaded on the upper graph.
- The learner changes: the marker position, by dragging.
- Caption: "Drag the marker. The shaded area above is the height of the curve below."

## the-two-ideas-connect / growth-of-accumulation

- Shows: a rate graph with the area up to a moving end shaded, a thin sliver at the end, and the slope of the running total beside it read off as the height of the rate at that end.
- The learner changes: the moving end and the width of the sliver.
- Caption: "Move the end of the area. The area grows by about the height of the graph times the step."

## from-rate-to-amount / a-steady-tap

- Shows: a tank beside the rate graph, with the water level read from the starting amount plus the shaded area so far.
- The learner changes: the starting amount, the rate and the time.
- Caption: "Change the start, the rate and the time. The level is the start plus the shaded area."

## from-rate-to-amount / a-moving-car

- Shows: a car on a short track with the velocity graph v(t) = 4 − 2t beside it, a counter for displacement and a second counter for distance driven.
- The learner changes: the time, with a play button.
- Caption: "Press Play. Displacement counts the backward stretch as negative and distance counts it as positive."
