# Figures wished for

Figures the first five lessons would use if the course had a diagram type for them. Each screen teaches without its figure.

## counting-outcomes

- Step `two-coins-four-results`: a list or tree of the four outcomes of two coin flips (HH, HT, TH, TT). The learner taps an event such as "at least one head" and the matching outcomes light up with a count over four. Caption: "Tap an event to see which of the four outcomes belong to it."

## when-the-condition-changes

- Step `a-clue-crosses-outcomes-off`: the same four-outcome list, with a button for the clue "at least one head" that crosses out TT and shows the count left. Caption: "Press the clue and watch the list shrink from four outcomes to three."
- Step `an-even-die`: a row of the six die faces where the learner picks a clue (even, odd, above 3) and the faces it rules out fade. Caption: "Choose a clue and see which faces can still have come up."

## independent-repetitions

- Steps `a-coin-and-a-die` and `multiplying-chances`: a grid of 2 rows (heads, tails) by 6 columns (die faces). The learner picks a coin result and a die result, the cell lights, and a line beneath shows 1/2 × 1/6 = 1/12. The existing outcome_grid only draws two dice with the same number of sides. Caption: "Pick a coin result and a die result. One cell out of twelve is lit."
- Step `any-number-of-events`: a strip of up to five coin flips, each with a heads chance of 1/2, where adding a flip halves the shaded share of a bar. Caption: "Add flips and watch the chance of all heads halve each time."

## centre-and-outliers

No gaps. The data_distribution figure covers every screen that needs one.

## same-centre-different-spread

- Step `distance-from-the-mean`: a dot plot of 1, 1, 1, 9 with a line from each dot to the mean and a square drawn on each line, so the squared distances can be seen as areas. The existing data_distribution figure shows the spread but not the squares. Caption: "Each square has sides as long as the dot's distance from the mean."
